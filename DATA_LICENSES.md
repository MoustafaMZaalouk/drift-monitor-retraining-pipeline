# Dataset licenses and attribution

No dataset files are committed to this repository. Both datasets are
downloaded at build time by `scripts/download_data.py` from the UCI Machine
Learning Repository, with pinned SHA-256 checksums, under the licenses below.

## Bike Sharing Dataset

- Authors: Hadi Fanaee-T, Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence and Decision
  Support (LIAAD), University of Porto.
- Citation: Fanaee-T, H. (2013). Bike Sharing. UCI Machine Learning
  Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
- Source URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/275/bike+sharing+dataset.zip
- Pinned SHA-256 (zip): b70182d0d0508e9abbb79306ce5c0cec34869000f8220175ac83d11dbe845401
- Underlying data: Capital Bikeshare system, Washington D.C., 2011-2012.

## Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset

- Citation: Sakar, C. O., Polat, S. O., Katircioglu, M., & Kastro, Y. (2018).
  Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
  https://doi.org/10.24432/C5F88Q
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
